Apex Talent

Child Safeguarding Policy

Draft version — August 2026
This is a starter draft describing how the platform is technically built to protect minors. It is not a substitute for a full safeguarding policy reviewed by a child-protection specialist, and should be treated as incomplete until that review happens.

Our approach

A meaningful share of athletes on this platform are under 18. Apex Talent Management's default posture is protective: a minor's profile is not publicly visible, and no highlight involving a minor is published, until a parent or guardian has explicitly and verifiably consented.

How this is enforced

  • Registering a player under 18 requires a named parent/guardian and their explicit consent — this cannot be skipped or defaulted.
  • Consent is timestamped, tied to the specific player, and revocable at any time by contacting us; revoking it removes the profile from public visibility immediately.
  • Guardian contact details are never shown publicly and are only accessible to Apex staff.

Scout and sponsor conduct

Scouts, clubs, and sponsors using the platform must route any interest in a minor through Apex staff rather than contacting the player or guardian directly. Any attempt to bypass this — requesting direct contact, private meetings, or personal information outside the platform's introduction process — will result in the organisation's access being suspended pending review.

Staff access

Access to guardian records and minor players' private details is restricted to authenticated staff accounts, and every moderation and verification action is logged for accountability.

Reporting a concern

If you believe a profile, highlight, or interaction on this platform puts a minor at risk, contact us immediately using the details that will be published here once the platform's safeguarding contact channel is finalised. Concerns involving imminent harm should also be reported to the relevant local authorities without delay.
