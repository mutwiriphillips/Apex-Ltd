The process

Three steps from unseen to scouted

The same path for every athlete, whether you play for a top-flight club or a county-division side.

01

Register your profile

Add your bio-data, sport, club or division, and playing history. Free, permanently, for every athlete.

02

Get verified & add highlights

Upload or link match highlights and let your club co-sign your record. Verified profiles get priority in scout searches.

03

Get scouted, sponsored, signed
