Privacy Policy

Draft version — August 2026
This is a starter draft written to match the platform's actual data practices. It has not yet been reviewed by a Kenyan lawyer or filed as part of Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) registration, and must not be treated as final or legally binding until that review is complete.

Apex Talent Management Ltd ("Apex", "we", "us") operates a player registration, video showcase, and athlete representation platform in Kenya. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Player profile data you provide at registration: name, date of birth, sport, position, club, county, division, and bio.
  • Contact details (email/phone), stored for verified scouts and staff only — never shown in the public directory.
  • For players under 18: a parent/guardian's name, relationship, and contact details, plus a record of the consent they gave and when.
  • Highlight video links and descriptions you or your club submit.
  • Basic technical data (IP address, timestamps) associated with consent records and account actions, kept for accountability under the Data Protection Act.

Why we collect it

To operate your public profile (where applicable), power scout/sponsor search and discovery, verify statistics and highlights, administer any representation agreement you enter into with Apex, and meet our legal obligations around minors' data and record-keeping.

Minors

Players under 18 do not appear in the public directory, and their highlights are not published, unless a parent or guardian has explicitly consented — recorded with a timestamp and revocable at any time by contacting us. Revoking consent removes the player from public visibility immediately.

Who we share data with

  • Verified scouts, clubs, and sponsors — limited to what's needed for a specific introduction, and never guardian or contact details without separate consent.
  • Federations and clubs you're affiliated with, for statistic verification purposes.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you (or a player's guardian) can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held about you, and can withdraw consent at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Data retention

We keep profile and consent records for as long as the account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal and dispute-resolution purposes. Deleting a player profile removes dependent personal data (guardian records, consents, and highlights tied to that profile).

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the Apex Talent Management data protection contact — details to be published here once the ODPC data controller registration referenced in the banner above is complete.
