Apex Talent

Terms of Use

Draft version — August 2026
This is a starter draft, not a finished legal document. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not yet reflect the FIFA Football Agent Regulations compliance structure described in the business plan. Do not rely on this as a binding agreement until it has been formally reviewed.

1. Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone who registers a player profile, submits a highlight, or uses the sponsor/ scout portal on the Apex Talent Management platform.

2. Open registration

Player registration is free and open to any competitive athlete in a covered sport. Registering does not create a representation relationship with Apex Talent Management — it creates a public (or, for minors, guardian-gated) profile only.

3. Accuracy of information

You agree to provide accurate information about yourself (or, for a guardian registering a minor, about the player). Apex reserves the right to remove profiles or highlights found to contain false or misleading statistics or claims.

4. Highlight content

By submitting a highlight video link or description, you confirm you have the right to share that content and grant Apex a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display and promote it on the platform. Submitted highlights are reviewed before appearing publicly.

5. Managed Athlete representation

Full-service representation (contract negotiation, transfer facilitation, sponsorship brokerage) is a separate, formal agreement entered into only with athletes selected for the Managed Athlete programme, and — for football — only involves FIFA-licensed agents where required by the FIFA Football Agent Regulations. Being listed in the open directory does not imply any such agreement exists.

6. Minors

Where a player is under 18, a parent or guardian must register on their behalf and provide consent as described in the Privacy Policy. Apex reserves the right to request verification of guardian identity.

7. Prohibited use

  • Impersonating another person or organisation.
  • Submitting fabricated statistics, achievements, or highlight content.
  • Using the sponsor/scout portal to contact players or guardians outside the platform's intended introduction process, particularly where a minor is involved.

8. Changes

These terms may be updated as the platform develops; material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
