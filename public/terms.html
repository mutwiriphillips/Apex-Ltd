Terms of use

Draft version — October 2026

Draft — not yet legally reviewedThis is a starter draft, not a finished legal document. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not yet reflect the FIFA Football Agent Regulations compliance structure described in the business plan. Do not rely on it as a binding agreement until it has been formally reviewed.

1. Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone who registers a player profile, uploads a photo or video, submits a highlight, or uses the scout and sponsor features on the Josriri platform.

2. Open registration

Player registration is free and open to any competitive athlete in a covered sport. Registering does not create a representation relationship with Josriri — it creates a public (or, for minors, guardian-gated) profile only.

3. Accuracy of information

You agree to provide accurate information about yourself (or, for a guardian registering a minor, about the player). Josriri reserves the right to remove profiles, photos or highlights found to contain false or misleading statistics or claims.

4. Photos, videos and highlights

You may upload only photos and videos of yourself (or, for a guardian, of the child you represent) that you have the right to share. You grant Josriri a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display and promote them on the platform. Every photo, uploaded video and highlight link is reviewed before it appears publicly, and we may decline or remove any of them. Uploads are limited in size and must be JPEG, PNG or WebP images and MP4, MOV or WebM videos.

5. Managed Athlete representation

Full-service representation (contract negotiation, transfer facilitation, sponsorship brokerage) is a separate, formal agreement entered into only with athletes selected for the Managed Athlete programme, and — for football — only involves FIFA-licensed agents where required by the FIFA Football Agent Regulations. Being listed in the open directory does not imply any such agreement exists.

6. Minors

Where a player is under 18, a parent or guardian must register on their behalf and provide consent as described in the Privacy Policy. Josriri reserves the right to request verification of guardian identity.

7. Introductions

Scouts, clubs and sponsors ask for an introduction through the platform. Josriri reviews each request and may decline it. Josriri does not promise that an introduction will lead to a trial, contract, sponsorship or any other outcome, and an athlete (or guardian) is free to ignore or decline any request.

8. Prohibited use

  • Impersonating another person or organisation.
  • Submitting fabricated statistics, achievements, photos or highlight content.
  • Uploading images or videos of other people without their permission.
  • Contacting players or guardians outside the platform's introduction process, particularly where a minor is involved.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated as the platform develops; material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
